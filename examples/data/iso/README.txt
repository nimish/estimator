The 2020–2022 workbooks contain hourly ISO New England data. The RI sheet
contains the Rhode Island series used by Giray Ogut, Bennet Meyers, and
Stephen Boyd in “Interpretable Net Load Forecasting Using Smooth Multiperiodic
Features” (2024). The paper uses three years of hourly Rhode Island net load
to evaluate point forecasts, hourly quantiles, and samples of future load.

Manuscript: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/multiperiodic_forecasting.html
Source notebooks and data: https://github.com/cvxgrp/smooth_multiperiodic_forecasting_experiments
